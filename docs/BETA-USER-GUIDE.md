## What ProofPack checks

ProofPack compares visible parts on a configured work surface with an approved kit recipe. It may ask you to separate overlapping parts, place a part flat or capture another image. It cannot check hidden contents, material composition, strength, torque or any property excluded by the recipe. Always manually verify every kit before packing during this supervised beta.
